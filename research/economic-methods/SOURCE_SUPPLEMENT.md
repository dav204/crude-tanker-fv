# Source observations after the parameter freeze

These observations were recorded after the first comparison was computed. They do
not change `assumptions.json`, the registered expectations or the displayed values.
They identify concrete work for a separately registered source-update leg. Finding
a disclosure does not establish that its figures have been reconciled into the model.

- **MPCC:** the [Q2 report](https://storage.mfn.se/36f4ce16-6b45-4c37-a890-eda439d37ef9/mpcc-q2-2026-financial-report.pdf)
  declares recurring DPS of $0.04 (page 3). It also discloses newbuild financing and
  a subsequent equity placement. The experiment's frozen generic payout and zero
  future financing cannot be certified without mapping the policy, debt draw dates,
  issued shares and payments into the opening balance sheet and forward schedule.
- **NAT:** the [Q2 report](https://ml-eu.globenewswire.com/Resource/Download/bb50c880-dc05-4bb1-b476-08bf0a9a2762)
  declares $0.27 for the quarter and describes distributing free cash flow. It reports
  $12.836m Q2 depreciation and $35.4m current long-term debt at June 30. The frozen
  discretionary earnings-ratio estimate and proxy D&A therefore remain diagnostics;
  an issuer cash-flow/distribution reconciliation is required.
- **CMDB:** [Costamare Bulkers' Q2 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2033535/000117184326005131/exh_991.htm)
  identifies a pending Bermondi disposal and separates adjusted from GAAP earnings.
  Its operating platform adds exposure beyond the owned-fleet strip. The release
  review does not establish a mechanical future payout policy. Continue to treat
  the frozen zero distribution as a forecast and reconcile the disposal/platform.
- **GNK:** the [Q2 statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326200/000114036126031442/ef20079211_ex99-1.htm)
  contains $22.367m Q2 depreciation. The preregistered $21m uses the older Q1
  observation explicitly; do not silently replace it after observing outcomes.

The frozen source table contains the other 22 policy references. The three checks
above close the *location/review* gap, not the remaining policy-to-accounting bridge
or balance-sheet reconciliation. No source absence is inferred from parser omission.
