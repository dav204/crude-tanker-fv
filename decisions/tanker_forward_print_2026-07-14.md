## Why this fires the trigger

The tanker forward curves + 12M TC lines are HELD at the 2026-06-07 war vintage (owner
option (i), 2026-07-02) precisely because "no market forward print existed" — one now
does. The $75k 3-yr term level sits **~46% below the held VLCC curve's ~$140k average**
(and 33% below the held 12M TC $111.5k): the term market is pricing substantial
normalization that the held curve does not carry. The front of the held curve, by
contrast, is roughly CORROBORATED by the Q3 bookings ($139.7k spot QTD vs held q1
$147.5k). Net: the hold's staleness is now **evidenced at the back, corroborated at the
front** — the exact shape question option (ii) was designed for.

## What is NOT done here

No curve or 12M TC line is touched — **curve promotion is human-only** (the same rule as
the 13-Jul dry-bulk FFA promotion, which was owner-ratified). The trigger sits at
`fired` (decision OWED, stays red/paged) until the owner rules.
