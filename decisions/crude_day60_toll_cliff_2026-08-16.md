## 1. Evidence sweep (2026-08-16, single-threaded per the standing web rule)

**The window and the two positions**

- Islamabad Memorandum signed ~17 Jun 2026; reopened Hormuz to commercial shipping
  **toll-free for 60 days**, i.e. expiring **~16 Aug** — today.
- **Iran's stated intent has been consistent and is on the record since day one.** Parliament
  Speaker Qalibaf, 18 Jun: "The Strait of Hormuz will not return to pre-war conditions," and
  Iran "would charge fees for services in the waterway after the grace period."
- **Iran draws a deliberate legal distinction**: the foreign ministry says it will *not*
  impose transit **tolls** but *will* levy **fees for services** (navigation, environmental
  protection), administered by the **Persian Gulf Strait Authority** (established ~5-6 May
  2026). That is the UNCLOS fig leaf — transit passage cannot lawfully be tolled; "service
  fees" is the framing chosen to survive that.
- **The US rejects both.** Trump has called any toll/fee a dangerous precedent, and on
  **Thu 13 Aug** said he plans to declare the strait **US territory** after "we finish
  defeating Iran" — met with an immediate retort from Tehran's deputy foreign minister.
  The parties are described as "at odds over control of the key waterway."

**The physical state of the waterway (UKMTO-attributed, CNN live coverage 15 Aug)**

- **Traffic: 17% of the pre-conflict average over the past seven days.**
- **US naval blockade still in effect**, barring ships to or from any Iranian port inside or
  outside the strait. One Iranian-flagged vessel inbound and one outbound in seven days.
- **Iran is attacking the traffic it proposes to bill.** Three attacks on ADNOC vessels
  inside 48h; an "unknown projectile" hit a bulk carrier's hull 14 Aug (crew safe); a vessel
  took "minor damage" from a drone attack Thu 13 Aug. Iran has claimed official
  responsibility and warned ships against attempting the crossing.
- The **Southern Omani route** — the alternative temporary corridor — is the highest-risk
  lane: **16 of 18** projectile strike incidents logged by UKMTO since 6 Jul.
- Flag mix through the strait: Panama and Liberia 12 each; **no US-flagged vessels**.
- UAE (Gargash) accused the IRGC of "piracy" and of using the strait as "a tool of economic
  coercion and blackmail."

**NOT established — stated plainly, because the absence is load-bearing**

- **No evidence Iran has actually imposed or begun collecting fees at expiry.** Intent is
  documented and consistent; *collection* is not.
- **No evidence of a toll-free extension or waiver.** Trump's "US territory" claim is the
  opposite of a negotiated waiver.
- **No evidence the Oman-brokered strait-management framework convened** — the carried-forward
  watch item from `crude_pause_talks_watch_2026-08-09.md`, whose branch-1 re-normalization
  PROPOSAL path was contingent on exactly that. It did not happen; branch 1 does not open.
